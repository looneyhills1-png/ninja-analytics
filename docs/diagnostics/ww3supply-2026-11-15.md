## Why this differs from the 15 November screenshot

The screenshot (5 indexed, 7 not indexed: 4 crawled-not-indexed, 1 blocked 4xx,
1 404, 1 redirect) is a Search Console **report**. Google offers no API that lists
the URLs behind it. Inspecting every URL Ninja Analytics can discover, none is
currently "crawled, not indexed", and the 19 indexed pages exceed the 5 the
screenshot shows, so the report was historical or covers different addresses.
The redirect category is reproduced exactly by `http://ww3supply.com/`. The
other categories could not be tied to specific URLs, and **no figure from the
screenshot is claimed as verified**.

## Per URL: should it be indexed?

* **`http://ww3supply.com/`**: no. It redirects to https; Google indexes the destination. Nothing to fix.
* **`https://www.ww3supply.com/`**: no. It serves HTTP 200 but its canonical is `https://ww3supply.com/`, so Google treats it as an alternate. Correct as is.
* **10 `/blog/<slug>/index.html` pages**: no. Each serves 200 with a canonical to the clean URL, none is in the sitemap, and Google has not even discovered them. They are reachable because the site's own links use them (34 links across 10 pages). Low urgency; the exact patch is in `docs/proposed-changes/ww3supply-clean-internal-links.md`.
* **`/blog`, `/index.html`, `/favicon.ico`**: not needed. `/blog` and `/index.html` return 200 with canonicals to the clean address; `/favicon.ico` returns 404 (the site has no favicon file; add one if you want it).
* **`/blog/community-first-72-hours/`**: yes. Live 200, 749 words, in the sitemap. Google knows it but has not crawled it yet. Other posts link to it through the `index.html` alias; pointing those links at the clean address and linking to it from `/blog/` and the home page is the useful step.
* **`/guides/best-wind-up-radios-uk/` (946 words) and `/supplies/` (375 words)**: yes. Live 200, in the sitemap (lastmod 12 and 14 Nov), indexable, not blocked, but unknown to Google. The page is not the problem; Google has not found it yet. Link to both from pages Google already indexes (home, `/guides/`) and ask Google to re-read the sitemap in Search Console. Google reports the sitemap was submitted 9 Nov and last downloaded 14 Nov with 0 errors. Ninja Analytics has read-only access and cannot do that for you.
